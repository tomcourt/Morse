To start or stop a lesson, click on the player control symbol or press Esc key.

Characters sent show up in the large result box. Black for correct, red for wrong, orange for timeout.

To see the morse for any character, hover the cursor over the character in the results table. To hear it, click on the character.

Remember to say outloud or clearly think of the character before typing it, otherwise you are training

your fingers to move when you hear a sound and will have trouble handwritting or hearing the words later.

To practice characters you are having trouble telling apart, set them in 'Enabled character set', to reset just select a lesson

Keep sessions short until you build up stanima.

Roll back the 'Koch enabled' and 'Lesson' when starting a new session to review the last characters you learned before learning any new characters.

Under 'Settings', 'Reset' controls how long after starting before morse starts and extra time whenever you miss a character to regain your composure
